## [Coordinating a region](https://lanej.io/charmed/organisms.html#coordinating-a-region)

Organisms combine controls, collections and visualizations into a reusable region. Each owns its UI behavior while the application supplies data and handles emitted events.

Use [templates](https://lanej.io/charmed/templates.html) to place regions into a screen, or explore [complete screens](https://lanej.io/charmed/screens.html) to see selection, focus and resizing coordinated across them.
